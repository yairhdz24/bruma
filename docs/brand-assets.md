# Brand and assets

- **Bruma icon** (`public/bruma-icon.png` and the sizes derived from it with `scripts/generate-brand-icons.py`): original artwork made for Bruma. © Yair Hernández, released with the project under the MIT License.
- **Sky** (`public/bruma-sky.jpg`): original image generated for Bruma. No stock photos, wallpapers or third-party screenshots.
- **Service logos** (`public/brands/`): official marks of Groq, OpenAI, Deepgram and Anthropic/Claude from [svgl](https://svgl.app), used only to identify each service. They belong to their owners and do not imply endorsement.
- **App icons on the pill and in history** are read at run time from the apps installed on your Mac; Bruma does not ship them.
- **Interface icons**: [Phosphor](https://phosphoricons.com) (MIT) and, on macOS, SF Symbols rendered by the system at run time (never bundled).
- **Typography**: the system font (San Francisco on macOS, Segoe UI on Windows). No fonts are bundled.
